## The thinking in one minute

The current page is **already good** — strong certifications, 571 reviews at 4.9★, real clinical backing. That's the trap: "not broken" leaves conversions on the table. The redesign is built around one principle — **answer the buyer's doubt before it's asked** — and the two doubts that actually block a marine-collagen sale:

1. *"Will it work, and when?"* → a **Week 2 / 4 / 8 / 12 results timeline** and an **8–12 week promise + 90-day money-back guarantee**.
2. *"Will it taste fishy?"* → an **"honest answers" section that leads with taste**.

Supporting moves: **Subscribe & Save** as the default offer, a **per-day price reframe** ("from ₹48/day"), a **sticky add-to-cart**, weaponised transparency (molecular-size science, marine-vs-bovine comparison), and concentrated social proof. All sequenced by **ICE** into two sprints, with an honest, A/B-validated impact range (not guarantees).

## Design direction

A deliberate **"deep-sea apothecary"** art direction — full-bleed marine photography, an editorial serif voice (Hedvig Letters Serif + Hanken Grotesk), a committed marine / bone / honey palette, hairline rules instead of card-grids, and asymmetric type-led composition. Chosen to feel like a premium clinical-beauty brand, not a templated supplement PDP.
