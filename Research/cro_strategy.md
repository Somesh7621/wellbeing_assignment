## 1. The product & the buyer

**Product:** Hydrolyzed Type I & III marine collagen peptides from wild-caught Korean/deep-sea salmon. Unflavored, 200g, ~₹1,529 (15% off ₹1,799). 4.9★ / 571 reviews. Dermatologist-formulated, <1000 Dalton peptides, "4x absorption," 20+ clinical studies, US-FDA-registered facility, FSSAI licensed, heavy-metal & mercury tested.

**Buyer:** Predominantly women 28–50, beauty-led (wrinkles, elasticity, hydration, hair, nails), increasingly men (per reviews). This is a **considered, skeptical, results-doubting purchase** with a delayed payoff (8–12 weeks). Two conversion battles dominate:
1. **"Will it actually work — and when?"** (efficacy doubt)
2. **"Will it taste/smell fishy?"** (marine collagen's #1 category objection)
Plus the usual: safety, value, vegetarian suitability.

## 3. Where conversions leak (the audit)

| # | Leak | Why it costs conversions |
|---|------|--------------------------|
| L1 | **Superlative, product-first headline** ("World's Finest… Powder") | Unsubstantiated superlatives raise skepticism; no outcome + timeline promise. |
| L2 | **No believable results timeline above the fold** (4–8 wks buried in FAQ) | The #1 objection ("will it work / when?") is never answered at the decision point. |
| L3 | **The fishy-taste objection is never addressed head-on** | The single biggest category-specific barrier is left to the buyer's imagination. |
| L4 | **No risk reversal / money-back guarantee** | First-time supplement buyers need a safety net for a delayed-payoff product. |
| L5 | **Subscription / Subscribe & Save not productised** | Leaves the highest-LTV, lowest-friction repeat mechanism on the table (Vital Proteins anchors on it). |
| L6 | **No cost-per-day / per-serving reframe** | ₹1,529 feels big; "~₹51/day — less than your coffee" reframes affordability. |
| L7 | **Above-the-fold overload** (14+ carousel images, tabs) with no tight proof→price→CTA composition | Cognitive load delays the decision; no single confident "hero moment." |
| L8 | **No sticky add-to-cart** on a long page | Intent decays every time the buyer has to scroll back up. |
| L9 | **Transparency shown as claims, not proof** (science + sourcing not visualised) | "4x absorption / <1000 Da" is a weapon presented as a footnote. |
| L10 | **Objections scattered across a 20-question FAQ** | Buyers won't dig; key objections must be surfaced and crushed. |
| L11 | **Reviews are text-only, no photo/video UGC surfaced** | UGC converts ~10x; verified-buyer + visual proof is missing. |
| L12 | **Generic CTA copy** ("Add to cart") | No value carried in the highest-attention button on the page. |

## 4. Strategy — 8 pillars

1. **Lead with a believable promise, not a superlative.** Outcome + timeline + proof: "Visibly firmer, smoother skin in 8–12 weeks — or your money back." Retire "World's Finest."
2. **Stack an irresistible offer.** Subscribe & Save (−20% + free shipping, cancel anytime) as the *default* choice; best-value 3-pack anchored; free gift; 90-day guarantee. Make one-time purchase the visibly worse deal.
3. **Compress the decision above the fold.** One confident hero: promise + 4.9★ proof + cost-per-day + guarantee + CTA in a single view. Add a **sticky buy bar**.
4. **Weaponise transparency.** Marine-sourcing story (deep-sea salmon), a molecular-science visual (<1000 Da → 4x absorption), a **marine-vs-bovine-vs-vegan** comparison, and lab reports as trust objects.
5. **Crush the 5 real objections explicitly:** taste/smell, results timeline, safety, vegetarian, value — each answered in its own beat, not buried in FAQ.
6. **Amplify social proof:** verified-buyer reviews, photo/video UGC, a results-over-time testimonial strip, "5M+ customers / 20+ clinical studies" credibility bar.
7. **Reverse the risk:** visible 90-day money-back guarantee tied to the 12-week protocol; easy subscription management.
8. **Mobile-first & fast:** sticky CTA, thumb-reachable controls, lazy imagery, no layout shift.
