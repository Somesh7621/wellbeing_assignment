The starting point

A genuinely good page. That's exactly the problem.

The current page isn't broken. It has real assets most competitors can't match: strong certifications, 571 reviews at 4.9★, and clinical backing. But "not broken" leaves money on the table. The buyer is skeptical, results-doubting, and worried about one thing above all: will it taste fishy and will it actually work? The page never answers those at the moment of decision.

The audit

What's earning trust vs. what's leaking sales.

Keep & amplify

  • Trust competitors can't match: FDA facility, FSSAI, 5 lab reports, mercury & heavy-metal tested
  • 571 reviews at 4.9★ with a believable 5★/4★ split
  • Real scientific edge: <1000 Da, Type I & III, salmon-sourced
  • Clear multi-pack discounting and a deep FAQ

Fix · where conversions leak

  • Superlative headline ("World's Finest") with no outcome + timeline promise
  • The fishy-taste & "when will it work" objections are never surfaced
  • No money-back guarantee; no Subscribe & Save; no per-day price reframe
  • No sticky cart; proof, science & objections buried below the fold
What actually blocks the sale

Five doubts stand between view and purchase.

1"Will it work, and when?" The delayed 8–12 week payoff creates doubt the page never resolves at the CTA.
2"Will it taste fishy?" Marine collagen's #1 category objection, left entirely to the buyer's imagination.
3"Is it safe / clean?" The answer exists in lab reports, but isn't framed as reassurance where it matters.
4"Is ₹1,529 worth it?" A lump sum with no per-day reframe and no risk reversal feels expensive.
5"Why should I trust this brand?" Proof is present but scattered, not concentrated into a confident moment.
Strategy

Eight moves, one principle: answer the doubt before it's asked.

i.

Promise, not superlative

Lead with outcome + timeline + guarantee.

ii.

Irresistible offer

Subscribe & Save as default; best-value cycle anchored.

iii.

Compress the decision

Proof + per-day price + CTA in one view; sticky bar.

iv.

Weaponise transparency

Molecular science + sourcing + comparison, made visual.

v.

Crush 5 objections

Taste, timeline, safety, veg, value, head-on.

vi.

Concentrate proof

Timeline of results + verified reviews as one moment.

vii.

Reverse the risk

Visible 90-day money-back promise.

viii.

Mobile-first & fast

Thumb-reachable CTA, lazy media, no shift.

The redesign, in moves

From "here's a product" to "here's your result."

Current page
  • "World's Finest… Powder" superlative headline
  • Results timeline hidden in a 20-question FAQ
  • Taste objection never mentioned
  • Flat 15% packs; no subscription, no guarantee
  • ₹1,529 shown as a lump sum
  • 14-image carousel + tabs = decision overload
Redesigned page
  • "Firmer skin, drawn from the deep sea" + 8–12 wk promise
  • Week 2 / 4 / 8 / 12 results timeline, front and centre
  • An "honest answers" section that leads with taste
  • Subscribe & Save (−20%) default + 90-day guarantee
  • Reframed to "from ₹48/day, less than a coffee"
  • One cinematic hero, sticky buy bar, clear path
